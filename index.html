THE EXPEDITION GAME

A decision-making game for teams, leaders, students, and explorers.

Choose an Expedition

Why It Matters

Good decisions rarely happen in perfect conditions. This game helps people think ahead, work together, and understand how pressure, communication, and planning shape what happens next.

How It Works

Step 1

Choose an expedition

Pick one of nine expedition and response modules, each with its own challenges, risks, and team pressures.

Step 2

Make decisions together

Work through scenarios where communication, judgement, and planning affect the path ahead.

Step 3

See what your choices change

Each decision shapes what happens next, revealing how teams respond under pressure.
