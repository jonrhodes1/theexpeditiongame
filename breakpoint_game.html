Operation Hielo Sur

January 2025  ·  Southern Patagonia Icefield, Chile  ·  2,400 metres

The Southern Patagonia Icefield — Hielo Sur — is one of the largest temperate ice masses on Earth. 350 kilometres of glacial terrain. Crevasse fields, whiteout conditions, and winds that change without warning. No roads. No rescue within six hours.

Your international team has assembled in Puerto Natales for what will be a 60-day crossing research expedition. The science is important. But this game is not about the science.

It is about what happens inside a team under sustained pressure. BREAK POINT uses the Dirty Dozen framework, twelve human factors identified in high-risk environments, to shape every decision you make. Most of them will be invisible until the debrief.

14 scenarios across 4 phases. Hidden factors accumulate with each decision. The debrief reveals what the team could not see in the moment.
