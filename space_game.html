Artemis IV - Lunar Surface Mission

Mission Day 1  ·  Shackleton Crater, South Lunar Pole  ·  Lunar Surface

The year is 2028. Your four-person crew has completed trans-lunar injection and is now in lunar orbit above the Shackleton Crater at the Moon's south pole. Mission objectives: establish the permanent Artemis Lunar Outpost foundation, conduct geological survey of polar ice deposits, and perform four critical EVAs across 14 surface days.

Your mission is Artemis IV. You are the fourth human crew to land on the Moon since Apollo 17 in 1972. Unlike Apollo, you will live and work in the Lunar Gateway habitat for 14 days - longer than any human has ever spent on the lunar surface.

Inspired by psychological documentation from Scott Kelly and Mikhail Kornienko's 340-day ISS mission, and the near-catastrophic Apollo 13 oxygen tank failure of 1970, this simulation places you inside decision-making pressure no simulation on Earth fully replicates. The Moon is 384,000 kilometres from the nearest surgeon. Every system failure is your problem. Every conflict happens in a space the size of a caravan.

14 scenarios across the mission profile. Several carry timers. Earlier decisions cascade through mission systems. Only teams with exceptional technical discipline and psychological cohesion will complete all objectives and return safely.
