Operation Toubkal

April 2021  ·  High Atlas Mountains, Morocco  ·  4,167 metres

The year is 2021. Your international expedition team has gathered in the mountain village of Imlil (1,740m) at the foot of the High Atlas. Your goal: summit Jbel Toubkal - North Africa’s highest peak at 4,167 metres - and return safely within 60 days, conducting psychological resilience research along the way.

Following the 2018 incident that made registered guides compulsory on Toubkal, your team has engaged Rachid, a veteran High Atlas guide with 14 years of experience. He knows these mountains in every season.

Spring in the Atlas is unpredictable. Morning sun gives way to afternoon cloud. Snow patches persist above 3,000 metres. A cold front is moving in from the north. The window to summit is narrow. The expedition begins at dawn.

14 scenarios across 60 days. Some decisions carry a 60-second timer. Earlier choices shape later crises. Only the most cohesive teams will summit.
